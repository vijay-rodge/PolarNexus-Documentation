# Social Media Content Generation

## 1. Multi-Platform Micro-Content

Generates tailored social media posts:
- **LinkedIn**: Professional research summaries, academic citations, and technological achievements.
- **X (Twitter)**: Concise factoids with official hashtags (`#NCPOR`, `#Antarctica`, `#MoES`).
- **Instagram**: Visual captions accompanying high-resolution expedition photographs.
