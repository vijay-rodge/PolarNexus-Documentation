# NPDC Scientific Datasets

## 1. Tabular Sensor Catalog

The National Polar Data Center (NPDC) maintains quality-controlled environmental sensor archives:
- **`maitri_aws_2012.csv`**: Hourly observations of air temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, wind direction, and barometric pressure at Maitri Station.
- **`bharati_weather_archive.csv`**: Continuous meteorological telemetry from coastal East Antarctica.
- **`himadri_arctic_aerosol.csv`**: Optical depth and black carbon aerosol loadings measured in Ny-Ålesund.
