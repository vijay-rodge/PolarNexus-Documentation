# Institutional Activities & Operations

## 1. Year-Round Polar Station Operations

Maintaining active scientific outposts in extreme polar environments demands rigorous year-round institutional schedules:
- **Summer Operations (November to March)**: Major construction, scientific field traverses, sediment coring, and team changeovers.
- **Winter-Over Operations (April to October)**: A dedicated wintering team of 20–25 scientists, doctors, and engineers maintains life-support systems, station heating, and automated atmospheric observatories through total polar darkness.
