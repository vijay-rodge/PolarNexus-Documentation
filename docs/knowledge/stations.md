# Polar Research Stations & Bases

## 1. Operational Stations Directory

India operates four primary research installations across the polar regions:

| Station Name | Geographic Location | Latitude | Longitude | Status | Scientific Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maitri** | Schirmacher Oasis, Antarctica | `70^\circ 45' 57'' 	ext\{ S\}` | `11^\circ 44' 09'' 	ext\{ E\}` | Active (Year-Round) | Atmospheric physics, geomagnetism, meteorology, paleoclimate. |
| **Bharati** | Larsemann Hills, Antarctica | `69^\circ 24' 28'' 	ext\{ S\}` | `76^\circ 11' 14'' 	ext\{ E\}` | Active (Year-Round) | Oceanography, satellite ground tracking, solid earth geophysics. |
| **Himadri** | Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard, Arctic | `78^\circ 55' 00'' 	ext\{ N\}` | `11^\circ 56' 00'' 	ext\{ E\}` | Active (Seasonal) | Arctic atmospheric chemistry, glaciology, marine biology. |
| **IndARC** | Kongsfjorden Fjord, Arctic | `78^\circ 59' 00'' 	ext\{ N\}` | `12^\circ 01' 00'' 	ext\{ E\}` | Active (Mooring) | Subsurface fjord oceanography, salinity, Arctic-monsoon teleconnections. |
| **Dakshin Gangotri** | Ice Shelf, Antarctica | `70^\circ 05' 37'' 	ext\{ S\}` | `12^\circ 00' 00'' 	ext\{ E\}` | Historic Monument | First Indian Antarctic base (decommissioned 1990 due to ice burial). |
