# Expedition Technical Reports

## 1. Institutional Report Series

The National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research publishes comprehensive Technical Publications following each annual campaign:
- **Technical Publication No. 1 through 35**: Comprehensive monographs compiling glaciological, geological, and biological reports.
- **Voyage Operational Summaries**: Logistical documentation recording vessel charter parameters, fuel logistics, helicopter flight hours, and waste disposal under the Antarctic Treaty Environmental Protocol (Madrid Protocol).
