# Research Projects & Scientific Themes

## 1. Core Polar Scientific Domains

Research endeavors are categorized across five core scientific disciplines:
1. **Cryospheric Glaciology**: Ice sheet mass balance, ice core drilling, radar sounding of subglacial topography.
2. **Polar Atmospheric Physics**: Ozone hole dynamics, greenhouse gas monitoring, Brewer spectrophotometry, katabatic boundary-layer wind physics.
3. **Physical & Chemical Oceanography**: Thermohaline circulation, Southern Ocean carbon sinks, nutrient fluxes, sea-ice extent dynamics.
4. **Geodesy & Solid Earth Geophysics**: Tectonic plate motion monitoring via GNSS/Doppler benchmarks, geomagnetic field reversals.
5. **Polar Biology & Extremophiles**: Microorganisms in subglacial lakes, lichen adaptation, penguin census telemetry.
