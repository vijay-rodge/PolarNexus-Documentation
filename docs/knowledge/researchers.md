# Researchers & Principal Investigators

## 1. Directory of Polar Scientists

PolarNexus indexes biographical and publication records for key scientists who have shaped India's polar legacy:
- **Dr. S.Z. Qasim**: Leader of the historic 1st Indian Expedition to Antarctica (1981–82).
- **Dr. Harsh K. Gupta**: Leader of the 3rd Expedition, overseen construction of Dakshin Gangotri in a record 60 days.
- **Dr. Rasik Ravindra**: Former Director of NCPOR, led the First Indian Scientific Expedition to the South Pole (2010).
- **Dr. M. Sarangapani**: Lead meteorologist for the 13th Antarctic Expedition (author of Item 754).
- **Surinderjit Singh**: Lead investigator for synoptic studies during the 5th Antarctic Expedition (author of Item 281).
