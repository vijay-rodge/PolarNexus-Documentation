# Scientific Publications Catalog

## 1. DSpace Publication Repository Integration

PolarNexus indexes and provides full-text semantic search over hundreds of peer-reviewed papers extracted directly from the NCPOR DSpace Institutional Repository.

### Notable Harvested Records:
- **Item 754**: *Analysis of Meteorological and Oceanological Situations Encountered during Summer Months of 1993 - 94 in Antarctica* (M. Sarangapani). Focuses on katabatic winds (80–90 knots), coastal fast-ice, and sea-ice facsimile charts.
- **Item 281**: *Typical Meteorological and Oceanological Situation Encountered during the Fifth Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica* (Surinderjit Singh). Focuses on cyclonic storms at 60°S-15°E moving to 62°S-35°E with 968 mb central pressure.
- **Item 133**: *Position Fixing and Geodetic Survey at Antarctica* (Survey of India). Details Doppler satellite tracking benchmarks for Maitri and Dakshin Gangotri.
