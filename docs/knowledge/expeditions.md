# Indian Polar Expeditions

## 1. Historical Chronology

Since December 1981, India has organized:
- **43+ Antarctic Expeditions**: Operating continuously from Dakshin Gangotri (1983) to Maitri (1989) and Bharati (2012).
- **16+ Arctic Expeditions**: Initiated in 2007, operating seasonal atmospheric and marine research at Himadri and IndARC.
- **12+ Southern Ocean Expeditions**: Dedicated oceanographic cruises on the *ORV Sagar Kanya* studying biogeochemical cycles between 40°S and 65°S.

```mermaid
timeline
    title Milestones of Indian Polar Expeditions
    1981 : 1st Antarctic Expedition : Led by Dr. S.Z. Qasim on 'Operation Gangotri'
    1983 : 3rd Antarctic Expedition : Dakshin Gangotri station established (Decommissioned 1990)
    1989 : 8th/9th Expedition : Maitri permanent year-round base established in Schirmacher Oasis
    2007 : 1st Arctic Expedition : India commences Arctic research in Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard
    2008 : Himadri Station : Permanent Arctic research station inaugurated
    2012 : Bharati Station : 3rd Antarctic station commissioned in Larsemann Hills
    2014 : IndARC Mooring : India's first multi-sensor underwater mooring deployed in Kongsfjorden
```
