# Expedition Explorer

## 1. Historic Expedition Voyages

Located at `pages/03_expeditions.py`, the Expedition Explorer catalogues 43+ Antarctic campaigns, 16+ Arctic voyages, and special Southern Ocean cruises:
- Filter by decade (1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s, Present).
- Details expedition commanders, chartered polar vessels (*e.g., MV Polar Circle, MV Magdalena Oldendorff*), participating institutions, and primary scientific breakthroughs.
