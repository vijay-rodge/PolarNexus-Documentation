# Faceted Search & Filtering System

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Facets

Enables fine-grained discovery across large polar archives:
- **Spatial Facet**: Antarctica (Schirmacher Oasis, Larsemann Hills), Arctic (Svalbard), Southern Ocean.
- **Temporal Facet**: Expedition seasons from 1981 to 2026.
- **Domain Facet**: Glaciology, Meteorology, Oceanography, Biology, Geodesy.
- **Format Facet**: PDF Reports, CSV Datasets, High-Res Images, Folium Maps.
