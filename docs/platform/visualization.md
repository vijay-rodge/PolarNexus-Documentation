# Scientific Visualizations Studio

## 1. Interactive Plotting & Charting

Located at `pages/05_analytics.py`, this studio allows researchers to generate responsive visualizations:
- Hourly timeseries line charts with rolling moving averages.
- Monthly temperature distribution box-plots and anomaly heatmaps.
- Wind roses and barometric pressure gradient correlation curves.
