# Station Explorer & Telemetry

## 1. Polar Observatories Directory

Located at `pages/02_stations.py`, this module details India's permanent and seasonal polar bases:
- **Geographic Coordinates**: WGS-84 normalized coordinates with interactive map markers.
- **Life Support & Construction**: Details energy systems (combined heat and power, solar arrays), environmental waste management under the Madrid Protocol, and laboratory amenities.
